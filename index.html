How to play

Ten guesses

Defuse the bomb by cracking its secret code in ten guesses or fewer. Each digit can be “0” to “9”, a code can start with “0”, and a code never repeats a digit.

Make a guess

Tap digits on the keypad to fill each space, then tap Enter. ⌫ deletes the last digit.

Read the lights

  • Green: a right digit in the right spot
  • Orange: a right digit in the wrong spot
  • Off: a digit that isn't in the code

Lights fill in from the top left: greens first, then oranges. They tell you how many digits are right, not which ones.

Cross out

Tap ✕ Cross out, then a digit, to put a red X on it as a reminder. Do the same again to clear it.

Levels

Easy codes have three digits, Medium four, Hard five and Expert six.

Quick Play

One bomb, ten guesses and no countdown. The clock just records how long you took.

Campaign

Defuse bomb after bomb. Level one gives you five minutes, and each level after that has fifteen seconds less. Under one minute, the bomb starts to flash. If the clock runs out or you use all ten guesses, it goes off and the campaign ends.

Concede

Tap Concede twice to give up and go back to the title screen. It counts as a loss.
