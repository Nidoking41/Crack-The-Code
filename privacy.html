Privacy Policy

Crack the Code · Last updated November 1, 2026

Crack the Code is a puzzle game. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any. If you use the leaderboards, your phone's own game service (Apple Game Center or Google Play Games) handles your scores.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your stats (games played, wins, streaks and times) and your color scheme choice on your own device so they're there next time you play. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Leaderboards

The leaderboards use Apple Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android. If you sign in to one of them, the game sends it your best streak for each mode and difficulty, and the leaderboard shows your Game Center or Play Games nickname next to it. That information goes to Apple or Google, not to us, and their privacy policies cover it: Apple and Google. You don't have to sign in to play, and you can sign out any time in your phone's settings or the Play Games app.

Internet use

The app works offline and does not need an internet connection to play. The leaderboards need one.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information, we collect none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
