Privacy Policy

Defuse the Bomb · Last updated October 28, 2026

Defuse the Bomb is a puzzle game. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your stats (games played, wins, streaks and times) and your color scheme choice on your own device so they're there next time you play. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet use

The app works offline and does not need an internet connection to play.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
